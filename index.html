🔒 Privacy

This public-safe version contains no real MP2 account numbers, personal contribution history, or personal birth years in the source code. Data entered here is saved only in this browser via localStorage when you click Save. Do not enter MP2 account numbers, MID numbers, passwords, bank details, or government IDs.
